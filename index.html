2024

The Beginning

It all started with a simple craving and a recipe scribbled on a napkin. We were just two people in a tiny kitchen, figuring out how to make the perfect cookie. The first batch was far from perfect — but it tasted like the start of something beautiful.

2025

Overnight Oats

When our mornings got busier with the little ones, we discovered the magic of overnight oats — a breakfast you prepare with love the night before. The kids loved picking their own toppings. It became our morning ritual, and soon friends were asking for jars of their own.

FAQ

Got questions? We have answers

Learn more about how we bake, deliver, and ensure freshness across Batam.

We take all orders exclusively through WhatsApp to ensure personal care and schedule fresh baking for you. Just click any "WhatsApp Us" button, and it will direct you to a WhatsApp chat with a pre-filled ordering format. Once we confirm your menu choice, we schedule your bakes.

We are a family home bakery located in Batam, Indonesia, and we provide same-day cold-chain delivery across the island. Deliveries are made from Monday to Saturday to ensure your overnight oats and cookies arrive perfectly chilled. Shipping fees vary depending on your district in Batam.

Our overnight oats stay fresh for up to 3 days when stored continuously in the refrigerator. We recommend keeping them cold until you are ready to shake and enjoy them. Do not freeze, as this can affect the texture of the oats and fruits.

Yes, all of our bakes and overnight oats are made with Halal-friendly ingredients and contain no refined sugar. We sweeten our oats naturally using local honey and fresh fruit. Our cookies are also baked with dark chocolate and organic whole grains.
