Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. If the two versions differ, the Indonesian version applies.

1. Data We Collect

  • Your name and WhatsApp number, when you verify.
  • e-Gift details: recipient name, message, orders and redemptions.
  • Your delivery address, if you give it to us over WhatsApp.

2. Payment Data

Online payments are processed by our payment provider. We keep the payment status and reference, but never your PIN, password or bank login.

3. How We Use It

We use your data only to verify your number, process and deliver orders, update you about them, and answer your questions. We do not sell your data.

4. Third Parties

We share what is needed with providers that help run this service: our payment provider, WhatsApp messaging service, couriers and hosting provider. An e-Gift buyer's name and message are shown to the recipient.

5. Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in for 30 days, plus browser storage for choices such as sound and your gift draft.

6. Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by WhatsApp +62 851-9029-9779, email hello@hanniel.co. We may need to keep some transaction records for bookkeeping as the law requires.
